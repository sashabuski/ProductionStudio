PROJECT INQUIRY

Hey! We’d love to learn more about what you’re creating so we can determine whether we’re the right production partner. As a next step, please review the information below and submit our Project Kickoff form.

Timing. We’re a hands-on production company focused on delivering exceptional craft, so we take on only a limited number of projects at a time. Because of this, last-minute turnarounds or rushed timelines are not typically feasible — we prefer to plan ahead to ensure the highest production quality.

Scope. Our core work includes creative development, cinematography, directing, full-service production, and post-production (editing, VFX, sound, and color). We’re open to unconventional or experimental requests (!) but generally focus on meaningful, high-impact projects rather than small fixes or minor content updates.

Budget. Production budgets vary depending on concept, locations, talent, crew size, and post-production needs. Our smallest engagements typically begin around $15k–$20k, with larger campaigns and multi-deliverable productions ranging from $50k–$150k+. If your expectations differ, you’re still encouraged to reach out — we’re happy to hear your ideas and discuss budget realities.

If you have questions or aren’t sure whether we’re a fit, please feel free to start a conversation with us anytime.
